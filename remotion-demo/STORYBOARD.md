# ShopIQ SaaS Demo Storyboard

Runtime target: **160 seconds**

Resolution: **1920x1080**

FPS: **60**

Composition: **SaaSDemo**

## Scene 1 — Cinematic Premium Intro, 0-10s

Introduce ShopIQ with the product logo, premium animated background, and positioning:

> A cleaner way to run the shop.

The scene establishes that ShopIQ is an AI-powered inventory and sales operating system for real shops.

## Scene 2 — Product Context, 10-25s

Explain who ShopIQ is for using three real-context cards:

- Owners
- Cash counters
- Managers

The goal is simple: ShopIQ brings everyday shop work into one workspace.

## Scene 3 — Problem / Pain Point, 25-45s

Show practical operational friction:

- Stock, sales, and payments split across separate work
- Paid invoices requiring manual payment follow-up
- Reports and decisions depending on guesswork

The scene transitions into a connected operational loop.

## Scene 4 — Product Reveal, 45-65s

Reveal the real ShopIQ dashboard in a premium browser frame.

Highlights:

- Business overview
- Quick actions
- Metric cards
- Chart containers
- Search/topbar/sidebar system

Animated chart overlays are used as motion support over the real dashboard frame.

## Scene 5 — Feature Walkthrough Part 1, 65-95s

Three feature beats:

1. Dashboard and quick actions
2. Product and inventory management
3. Invoice and billing workspace

Uses light-mode product screenshots so users see the app clearly and understand the workflow.

## Scene 6 — Feature Walkthrough Part 2, 95-125s

Three deeper feature beats:

1. Payments and purchases
2. Business PDF reports
3. ShopIQ Copilot

Uses real dark and light screenshots with layered product-frame motion.

## Scene 7 — End-to-End Workflow, 125-145s

Animate the connected business flow:

Add product -> Create invoice -> Payment syncs -> Stock updates -> Reports and Copilot

Also shows key guardrails:

- No partial invoice if stock fails
- Walk-in customers pay on spot
- AI writes need approval

## Scene 8 — Trust / Polish, 145-153s

Show dark liquid mode and light classic mode side by side.

Highlights:

- Role-aware modules
- Confirmation-gated AI writes
- PDF reports and activity history
- Theme support

## Scene 9 — Final Value / CTA, 153-160s

Final brand moment:

> Premium shop operations. Built for daily work.

Closing line:

> Leave the 90s software behind. Run the shop from one premium operating system.
