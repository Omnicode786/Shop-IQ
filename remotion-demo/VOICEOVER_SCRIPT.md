# Voiceover Script

Approximate duration: **160 seconds**

## 0-10s — Intro

ShopIQ is a cleaner way to run the shop.

It brings inventory, billing, payments, reports, and AI assistance into one modern operating system for real retail work.

## 10-25s — Context

For owners, ShopIQ keeps the whole business in view.

For staff, it keeps daily billing fast and organized.

For managers, it connects stock, dues, purchases, and reports without forcing the team through scattered screens.

## 25-45s — Problem

Most shop software still feels stuck in the past.

Sales happen in one place, stock is checked somewhere else, payments are tracked manually, and reports become a separate chore.

ShopIQ is built around the relationships that matter, so the same action can update the right records across the system.

## 45-65s — Product Reveal

The dashboard starts with what the user needs next.

Quick actions sit beside live business numbers, recent activity, stock risk, invoices, and reporting signals.

Instead of opening five separate tools, the user begins from one command center.

## 65-95s — Feature Walkthrough Part 1

Inventory is structured for real shop operations.

Each product can carry SKU, barcode, category, supplier, pricing, stock quantity, reorder level, and batch details where needed.

Billing stays invoice-driven.

When an invoice is created, ShopIQ tracks the customer, items, payment state, stock movement, and remaining balance in one connected flow.

## 95-125s — Feature Walkthrough Part 2

Payments and purchases do not float away from the records they belong to.

Customer receipts connect back to invoices. Supplier payouts connect back to purchases.

Reports turn the same operational data into clean PDF documents.

And ShopIQ Copilot can answer business questions, prepare reports, and draft database actions, while asking for confirmation before it writes.

## 125-145s — Workflow

The workflow is simple.

Add a product.

Create the invoice.

Record the payment.

Move the stock.

Review the report, or ask Copilot for the next step.

Every part of the system is designed to keep the business record consistent.

## 145-153s — Trust and Polish

ShopIQ supports role-aware access, activity history, confirmation-gated AI actions, and multiple interface modes.

Dark liquid glass when you want depth. Clean light mode when you want clarity.

## 153-160s — Final

Leave the 90s software behind.

Run the shop from one premium operating system.

ShopIQ.
